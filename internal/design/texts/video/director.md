# Video: how to run the work

These are steps to choose from, not a chain to walk. Decide from the conversation which ones this video needs and say your scale in
one line ("You gave a script and the style comes from the design system: going straight to a draft"). Skip a step when its answer is
already there. Never restart the process: "change the second scene" changes that scene only; "another story" goes back to the story
and keeps what still fits.

| Step | What comes out | Needed when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief | `brief.md`: goal, audience, where it plays, length, what to keep from the material | Something of this is missing. Ask only what the talk and the material do not answer, seven questions at most, each with a default |
| Three stories | three paragraphs in the chat | No idea is given and the video is longer than about 15 s |
| Script | `script.md`: per scene what is seen, said and how long. Turn adjectives into numbers: "lively" is a duration, an ease and a stagger | Nearly always, even if it is very short |
| Style frames | 2-3 directions, one still each | Neither the design system nor a reference sets the look |
| Animatic | key frames on the time line, with the voice if recorded | The video is long, costly or follows a voice |
| Animation | the scenes | Always |
| Sound | voice, music, effects on the moments | Not in this version |
| Check | see `{{cli}} design video guide check` | Always |
| Render | a draft by yourself, the final when the person asks | Always a draft before you say it is ready |

Stop and ask one question only where going on is costly and there is a real fork: no idea, a long video, an important showing, a number
you cannot confirm.

Rules that do not bend:
- **Never invent a fact.** Numbers and names come from the material; what you cannot confirm is marked `NEEDS VERIFICATION` in the
  script and said out loud to the person.
- **The check always runs**, at any scale, and you say "ready" only after you looked at the frames.
- **Edit by name:** a scene and a block, not a rebuild. Fix the text before you animate it. For a long video show the first 20-30 seconds first.
- **Before animating a long one,** say the cost: scenes, seconds, render time. A frame costs about 0.25 s, so a minute at 30 fps is about 8 minutes.
- **Material is the main input.** Attachments, documents and boards the person gave outrank what you imagine.
- **No outside providers:** no generated video, no text-to-speech, no stock over an API. Everything is drawn here.
